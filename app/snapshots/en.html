Our method: value, cleanliness and efficiency

Assessment and preliminary study

We visit your home or business to analyse orientation, insulation and usage. With this data we run a thermal study and propose the most efficient option so you only pay for the capacity you actually need.

Choosing the right system

We compare split, ducted or cassette systems from leading brands. We evaluate performance and consumption to offer the most cost-effective solution within your budget.

Clean and safe installation

Certified technicians protect your space, install with precision and remove all waste. We deliver RITE certificates and back our installation with a 3-year workmanship warranty.

Maintenance and after-sales

We carry out periodic checks, filter cleaning and fast assistance for breakdowns to extend service life and keep the system efficient.
